• Tap BONK to swing your hammer
• Crack blocks for coins, push them into your crusher for team points

Cute block pets pop out of broken blocks and boost your coins. Hunt for rare Shiny and Rainbow ones!

• BONK PARTY every Saturday 18:00 UTC: nonstop Mega Blocks, x2 coins, x3 Blocklings!

GET STRONGER
Upgrade your hammer, Rebirth for permanent boosts, claim daily rewards and finish quests.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Every friend in your server gives +10% coins (up to +50%).
